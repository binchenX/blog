you are not your job<br>
you are not your social media post<br>
you are not others' expectation or gossip<br>

you are your regret<br>
you are what you read<br>
you are the tears you can't hold in wind<br>

don't expect the phrase<br>
you don't need that raise<br>
when you escape people's gaze you feel like in paradise<br>

you are the constant struggle<br>
you are the occasional despair and sorrow<br>
you are the forgotten dilemma you can't think of how to handle<br>
