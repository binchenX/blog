I planned to write a book called [Cloud Native](https://landscape.cncf.io/)[^1] Primer last year. O'Reilly declined [the proposal][proposal] because they preferred deep dives into specific topics rather than a broad overview. And they were right.

I intended to self-publish it, as I did with [Understand Containers](https://www.skyx.au/understand_container/).

But with the advancements in large language models (LLMs), I no longer think it's worthwhile to write it. Actually, I feel most technical books and blogs are not worth writing (and reading[^2]) in the LLM era. People can learn *much more*[^3] effectively using LLMs.

So, this is my wrap-up for the will-never-be-finished [Cloud Native Primer](https://www.skyx.au/cloudnativeprimer/).
